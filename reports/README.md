# Published findings

**[GMC-Verifier_Findings_102-systems_2026-10-26.pdf](GMC-Verifier_Findings_102-systems_2026-10-26.pdf)**
- 40 pages. Every third-party MetaTrader 5 system assessed to date, one entry each.

## What is in it

102 systems. 142 assessment runs. **Not one cleared every check.**

| verdict | systems |
|---|---|
| Not viable as presented | 69 |
| Incomplete - the report did not carry what the check needs | 28 |
| Not evaluable on the market tested | 5 |
| **Cleared every check** | **0** |

Each entry carries that system's own measured figures - its return, the return of simply
buying and holding the same instrument over the same window, its worst drawdown, how much of
its profit came from its best few trades, how much adverse fill its edge can absorb, and the
position sizes it actually used - followed by the findings in plain sentences.

## What is deliberately not in it

**No system is named.** Not the product, not its author, not its vendor, not its storefront,
not its listing. Each system is referred to by a number assigned in register order. The
purpose of this document is to show what assessment finds across a whole market, not to
single out an individual seller - and a verification business that publishes a hit list is
not a verification business.

**No threshold is published.** A reader learns what was asked of each system and what was
found. The level each verdict was drawn against, and the arithmetic behind it, are part of
the tool and are not published - see [../TERMS_OF_USE.md](../TERMS_OF_USE.md).

**Nothing is estimated.** Where a report did not carry what a check needs, the entry says
*not evaluable* and says why. That is a result, not a gap, and it is never filled in with a
guess - a guessed figure reads like a measurement and is a statement about the guess.

## How to check it

Every figure in it comes from the assessed system's own Strategy Tester report. If you are
the author of one of these systems and want to know which entry is yours, see
[../PRIVACY_AND_DISCLOSURE.md](../PRIVACY_AND_DISCLOSURE.md) - I will tell you, and I will
send you your own full assessment. If a figure in it is wrong, open a correction:
[../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/correction.yml](../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/correction.yml).

The method the findings were produced under is
[../docs/methodology.md](../docs/methodology.md), and the checks themselves are listed in
[../docs/what-it-checks.md](../docs/what-it-checks.md).
