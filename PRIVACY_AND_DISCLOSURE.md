# Privacy & Disclosure — GMC EA VERIFIER

**Last updated: 8 October 2026**

## 1. What This Covers

Two separate things:

1. **Privacy** — what happens to anything you send, and what happens in this public repository.
2. **Disclosure** — the things you are entitled to know about me before you trust a number I give
   you, including where my interests and yours could diverge.

## 2. This Repository Collects Nothing

- ❌ No analytics, no tracking, no cookies, no scripts
- ❌ No mailing list, no sign-up, no contact form
- ✅ Static documents only

**GitHub itself logs visits** under [its own privacy
policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement),
which I do not control and cannot see beyond public star and fork counts.

**Anything you post in Issues or Discussions is public and permanent.** Deleting a comment does not
reliably remove it — it persists in caches, email notifications and archives. See
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for the three things never to post.

## 3. If You Send Me An EA To Verify

This is the part that matters, so it is stated plainly.

**What I will do:**
- ✅ Treat your EA, your reports and your identity as **confidential by default**
- ✅ Return your results **to you only**
- ✅ Delete your files on request, and confirm when it is done

**What I will NOT do:**
- ❌ **Name you or your EA publicly. Ever. Not even to say it passed**
- ❌ Publish, share, resell or redistribute your source code or your reports
- ❌ Discuss your submission in a public thread, even anonymously, without your written permission

**On aggregate findings:** I may publish *patterns* observed across many submissions — for example
*"three grid EAs by three different authors shared the same unbounded-exposure structure"* — in a
form that **names nobody and identifies no submission.** If a finding cannot be anonymised, it does
not get published.

**Do not send me anything in a public Issue or Discussion.** Nothing about your EA belongs in
public, and I will not read unsolicited source code posted there.

## 4. ⚠ DISCLOSURE: I TRADE MYSELF

**This is the obvious conflict and you should hear it from me rather than wonder about it.**

I run my own Expert Advisors on my own accounts. So the fair question is: *if you send me an EA and
it turns out to be good, what stops me from simply trading it?*

**My commitments:**

- ❌ **I will not trade your strategy, copy its logic, or build a derivative of it.**
- ❌ **I will not pass it to anyone else, at any price.**
- ✅ **I will tell you if your EA closely resembles something I already run**, because that is a
  conflict you are entitled to know about before you decide whether to send it.
- ✅ **If a submission would put me in a position I cannot honestly hold, I will decline the work
  and say why** rather than take the fee.

**And the honest context, which is the strongest assurance available:** my own research record is
**12 EAs built, 10 failed their own pre-registered bar, around 164 trials, 0 passes.** The scarce
and valuable thing here is the *verification discipline*, not strategies — and the fastest way to
destroy a verification business is to be caught taking a client's edge.

**Verify this rather than trust it:** nothing in my published findings names a third party's EA,
and nothing ever will.

## 5. On Other People's Products

**No third party's EA, author or vendor is named anywhere in this repository, and none will be.**

If asked in public whether some named commercial EA is a scam, I will not answer, because:

- a figure that does not reproduce is **usually a mistake, not a fraud**, and I cannot tell which
  from the outside
- an author deserves to be asked before adverse findings about their work are published
- and I have made four errors of exactly that kind in my own work, documented in this repository

**Mechanisms get discussed. Products do not.** That is more useful to you anyway, because it
transfers to the next EA you look at.

## 6. What My Figures Are, And Are Not

- **Every published figure is a measurement on my own accounts, my own EAs, my own broker.**
  Reported as what was measured — not as a general truth.
- Your broker, symbol, account currency and platform build will differ. **Check your own.**
- **A clean verdict means "not yet disqualified".** No backtest can prove an EA works.
- **Not financial advice.** Not a recommendation to trade. **Trading carries risk of loss.**
